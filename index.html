Company Overview

Built so businesses of every size get enterprise-grade infrastructure.

Capital Group of Companies Lesotho is an information technology solutions provider specialising in enterprise IT infrastructure, data centre design, cloud and virtualisation services, networking, cybersecurity, and managed IT support. We serve small, micro, and medium enterprises, public sector institutions, and large corporates — locally and internationally.

Our team has delivered infrastructure, virtualisation, and communications projects for government agencies, financial institutions, utilities, and private-sector clients across multiple countries — a track record built on real, delivered outcomes rather than promises. Our approach combines deep technical expertise with a customer-first mindset: we design solutions around each client's real business needs rather than offering one-size-fits-all technology.

In Detail

A full infrastructure stack, under one provider.

From the physical data centre floor to multi-cloud and end-user devices — open a module for the full breakdown.

MODULE 01
Enterprise IT Infrastructure & Data Centre Services
Complete data centre solutions across three layers: physical, active equipment, and advanced professional services.
Physical Infrastructure
  • Fire detection and suppression systems
  • Physical access control — biometric, facial recognition, card-based
  • IP-based CCTV and surveillance systems
  • Raised access floors and ceilings, environmental monitoring
  • Structured cabling (fibre and copper)
Active Equipment
  • Enterprise servers and storage — NAS, SAN, SMB/NFS, block/file/object
  • Disk- and tape-based backup, including deduplication and VTL
  • Core networking — routers, switches, load balancers
  • Next-generation firewalls and IPS, built around zero-trust principles
  • UPS, automatic voltage regulators and intelligent PDUs, energy-efficient designs
  • Precision cooling — in-row, in-room, and floor-standing
Advanced Professional Services
  • Server consolidation and virtualisation — Hyper-V, VMware vSphere/vCenter
  • Hybrid and private cloud infrastructure consulting
  • Storage optimisation, consolidation, disaster recovery/replication
  • Exchange, SharePoint and Active Directory design — on-prem and via Microsoft 365
  • Desktop and application virtualisation — Citrix, VMware Horizon
MODULE 02
Cloud Services
Planning, migrating to, and managing public, private, and hybrid cloud environments.
  • Microsoft Azure — VMs, Azure Virtual Desktop, Azure SQL, Microsoft 365 integration
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud hosting, storage, infrastructure migration
  • Google Cloud Platform (GCP) — cloud infrastructure and workload deployment
  • Cloud migration strategy — lift-and-shift and hybrid-cloud approaches
  • Cloud backup, disaster recovery, and cost-optimisation reviews
  • Ongoing cloud management and support
MODULE 03
Virtualisation & Enterprise Applications
Modernising core business applications and on-premises infrastructure.
  • Microsoft Exchange Server and Exchange Online (Microsoft 365)
  • Microsoft SharePoint Server and SharePoint Online
  • Microsoft SQL Server and cloud database platforms
  • Microsoft Hyper-V virtualisation
  • Remote Desktop Services and Azure Virtual Desktop
  • VMware vSphere, vCenter, and Horizon (VDI)
  • Private and hybrid cloud platforms and orchestration
MODULE 04
Networking & Connectivity
LAN design, implementation and certification, extending to WAN integration.
  • Wired and wireless (Wi-Fi) network design
  • Structured cabling (fibre and copper)
  • Next-generation firewalls and intrusion prevention
  • Load balancers, routers, and switches
  • Network security aligned with zero-trust principles
MODULE 05
End-User & Client Device Lifecycle
Selection, deployment, and ongoing maintenance of end-user computing.
  • Business and enterprise desktops
  • Business and enterprise notebook computers
  • Workstations (HP and Dell)
  • Modern business tablets and mobile computing devices
  • Microsoft 365 collaboration — Exchange Online and SharePoint Online integration
MODULE 06
Server & Storage Solutions Consulting
Pre-sales through installation, configuration, and maintenance.
  • Network Attached Storage — NFS, SMB/CIFS, HTTP, WebDAV, FTP
  • Storage Area Networks — iSCSI, Fibre Channel, FCoE
  • Disk-based backup with deduplication, and tape-based backup
  • Storage replication and disaster recovery
  • Storage integration for virtualised and cloud environments
  • Storage integration for SQL Server and Exchange
MODULE 07
Power & Precision Cooling
Clean, stable power and precision cooling for comms closets, server rooms and data centres.
  • Power design, implementation, and consultation with a focus on energy-efficient equipment
  • Alternate power supply partnerships (e.g. generators) for complete turnkey solutions
  • In-row, in-room, and floor-standing precision cooling
  • Delivered with specialist partners for refrigeration piping and installation
MODULE 08
Security Systems: Alarms & CCTV
Intruder alarms and camera systems for homes and businesses.
  • Hardwired or wireless intruder alarm systems with on-site alarm and mobile/security-company alerts
  • Wireless and wired CCTV camera systems
  • Control room setups
  • Network video recording with local and remote viewing
MODULE 09
Software Development & Digital Solutions
Mobile, web and desktop applications, plus messaging solutions.
  • Mobile applications — native and cross-platform, fleet management and tracking systems, custom business apps
  • Web and desktop application development, including Point-of-Sale systems
  • Web hosting and domain registration
  • Tiered support SLAs — 1hr critical, 2hr medium, 4hr low-priority response
  • SMS and messaging-gateway solutions
MODULE 10
Business Analysis & IT Consultancy
Structured analysis guiding clients through an iterative solution-development approach.
  • Identifying strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities through structured business analysis
  • Requirements analysis and design
  • Implementation, testing, and continuous improvement
MODULE 11
Sales, Repairs & Technical Expertise
Hardware sales and repair, backed by a multi-platform technical team.
  • Sales and repair of desktops, servers, laptops, printers, routers, switches, smartphones, tablets, monitors, and peripherals
  • Technical team experienced across Microsoft, Linux, UNIX, and Cisco-based systems
  • Hardware expertise in Dell, HP, and IBM equipment
  • Regular technician refresher training and a well-stocked spare parts inventory
MODULE 12
Managed IT & Maintenance Services
Onsite support and break/fix maintenance, with standard performance reporting.
Onsite Support
  • Certified, trained technicians available at customer sites
  • Installation and de-installation of hardware, software and peripherals
  • Equipment upgrades, configuration, and physical relocation
  • Software installation, upgrades and patching — in and out of warranty
  • Standard monthly reporting — SLA performance, root cause and trend analysis
Break/Fix Maintenance
  • 24/7/365 support options
  • Vendor-supplied and customer-owned equipment, in and out of warranty
  • Service requests accepted via email; on-site troubleshooting with sparing-pool options
  • Nationwide coverage with certified technicians
  • Standard image reload and data transfer for inoperable units
